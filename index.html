Why Choose Swapna Jyothi Solar?

A rooftop solar setup is a 25-year power partnership. We engineer every kilowatt honestly, execute installations to rigorous structural standards, and stand by your system long after synchronisation.

The Swapna Jyothi Advantage

Maximum Tariff Offsetting & Bill Reduction

Every kilowatt installed generates 100–140 units of clean energy per month, targeting and wiping out your highest slab charges first to maximise immediate monthly savings.

Turnkey PM Surya Ghar Subsidy Clearance

We handle the bureaucratic heavy lifting, filing your portal application and driving it through to final account credit, securing up to ₹78,000 in direct government subsidies for residential systems.

Seamless DISCOM Net-Metering Integration

Export surplus power back to the grid and watch your meter run in reverse. We handle end-to-end liaison with your local DISCOM (TGSPDCL / TGNPDCL) for rapid net-meter approval and meter installation.

High-ROI Commercial & Industrial Power

Slash operational overhead, lock in fixed daytime power tariffs, and take advantage of accelerated tax depreciation benefits to shorten your capital payback window.

Heavy-Duty Engineering Built for Indian Climates

Tier-1 high-efficiency panels, hot-dip galvanised rust-proof mounting structures, and dedicated multi-stage lightning and earthing protection, engineered to withstand extreme heat, monsoons and high wind loads.

Dedicated Local Maintenance & Lifetime Support

One call handles routine panel cleaning, thermal performance audits and preventive servicing, supported by our comprehensive installation warranties and structured Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC).

Direct Government Benefits, 100% Fully Managed

Lock in maximum capital incentives before guidelines update. The Central Government directly offsets your initial setup costs with pre-approved Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) subsidy slabs. Pick a system size to see your estimate.
